Checked 7 November 2026. No customer data is included in this repository.

Voucherify Organization lists at US$1,300 per month on monthly billing, equivalent to US$15,600 for twelve months. Business lists at US$650 per month. These are public US list prices, not a verified NZ or AU invoice. Organization lists 50,000 monthly calls, a 200-per-minute burst and five projects; Business lists 25,000 calls, 100 per minute and three projects. Both list unlimited users and campaigns. Enterprise is quoted. Source: https://www.voucherify.io/pricing

The vendor's Bellroy case study establishes Australian retail relevance, not an Enterprise DNA customer or a known invoice: https://www.voucherify.io/customers/bellroy

The available Command Centre customer and CRM snapshots contained no Voucherify usage record. Live CRM was not consulted. Buyer evidence remains 2/5. Alternative-search listings were present on G2 and other comparison sites; no keyword-volume measurement was available. Search score 3/5 is a qualitative estimate, not measured demand. https://www.g2.com/products/voucherify/competitors/alternatives

Selected scores in order: annual bill 2, data-shaped 5, buyer evidence 2, search demand 3, one-session buildable 4, import path 5: 21/30. Coupon Carrier scored 18 but its base annual bill was below the five-figure threshold. Kentik scored 18, with continuous network telemetry outside this session's build scope. Parashift scored 14, with document-recognition dependencies. Pricing references: https://www.couponcarrier.io/pricing/ ; https://www.kentik.com/product/plans-pricing/ ; https://parashift.ai/en/pricing/

The registry already has loyalty points, wholesale trade promotions and generic business rules. This new family is discount coupon operations: finite code limits, percentage or fixed discounts, local order eligibility and use/reversal records. It has no point accrual, stored value, retailer rebates or customer distribution network.

Voucher CSV definitions and examples: https://docs.voucherify.io/api-reference/exports/create-export . The synthetic fixture follows the documented headers. It has not been validated against a private customer's actual export. Raw import fields remain available for reconciliation. Vendor rules outside the supported local subset are not executed.

The ten README questions describe analysis the base runs today. We have not established that Voucherify cannot answer them; no such claim is made. The custom terms and review evidence make them specific to an owner's business.
